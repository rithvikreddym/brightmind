About

BrightMind is a small, free website for students aged 14 to 22 who feel stressed about school. It has four short activities: breathing, meditation, mindfulness and calming music. No accounts, no forms, and nobody asks for your name.

I made it for the in-between moments, like the break before a lesson or a pause halfway through revision.

The four activities

  • Breathing (3 min). Follow a growing and shrinking circle: in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, out for 6.
  • Meditation (4 min). A guided session with calm sound that talks you through each step.
  • Mindfulness (3 min). A 5-4-3-2-1 exercise that moves your attention to what you can see, feel, hear, smell and taste.
  • Calming Music (5 min). Choose a soft track, set the volume and listen.

Who it is for

It is for anyone feeling the pressure of exams, homework and deadlines. Teachers, parents and school counsellors are welcome to point students to it as a quick, safe way to take a break.

Coins and streaks

Finish an activity and you earn 10 coins. Do at least one a day and the next circle on your 7-day streak gets filled in. It all stays on your own device, so there is nothing to log into.
