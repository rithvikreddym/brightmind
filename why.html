Why Us

School pressure is real

Exams, assignments, deadlines and everyone's expectations add up. The World Health Organization reported in 2024 that 63% of 15-year-old girls and 43% of 15-year-old boys felt pressured by schoolwork.

That kind of stress can hurt concentration, motivation and sleep. And most of us are too busy to work out which stress-relief methods actually help, or to hunt for them in different places.

Why not just use Headspace or Calm?

They are good apps. But they hold so much that it is hard to choose when you only have a few minutes, and some parts need a subscription. BrightMind goes the other way.

  • It is made for students aged 14 to 22, and for school stress in particular.
  • It is free. Nothing is behind a payment or an upgrade.
  • Every activity takes 5 minutes or less.
  • You get four choices and one click to start. No account to make.
  • Your coins and streak stay on your own device. Nobody asks for a name, address or phone number.
  • Coins and a 7-day streak give you a small reason to come back.

If it feels bigger than this

BrightMind is just a set of simple relaxation activities, not medical advice. If stress feels too big to handle, please talk to a parent, a teacher or your school counsellor.
